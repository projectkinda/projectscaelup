Common questions

Why does it need the camera?

To check you're still at your desk during a focus session. Video is analysed live on your iPhone and is never saved or sent anywhere.

Why does it need Screen Time?

So it can notice when you open the apps you chose and lock them after a session. Apple keeps your picks private; the app can't see which apps you picked.

I tapped "Don't Allow" on Screen Time.

Open Settings > Screen Time, or tap Ask again in the app. You can use the timer and camera check without it.

An app is locked and I need it.

Locks from a finished session end on their own (10 minutes plus 2 per distraction, up to 60). Locks apply only to the apps you flagged.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything is stored on your iPhone only.
