Common questions

Why does it need the camera?

To check you're still at your desk during a focus session. On iPhone, video is analysed live and never saved. On Android, a small temporary still is analysed on your phone and deleted straight away. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Why does it need Screen Time? (iPhone)

So it can notice when you open the apps you chose and lock them after a session. Apple keeps your picks private; the app can't see which apps you picked.

I tapped "Don't Allow" on Screen Time.

Open Settings > Screen Time, or tap Ask again in the app. You can use the timer and camera check without it.

Why does Android need Accessibility and "display over other apps"?

Accessibility tells the app which app has come to the front, so it can track and block the apps you flagged. It does not read your screen or what you type. The overlay draws the lock screen over a blocked app. You can turn either off any time in Android Settings.

An app is locked and I need it.

On iPhone, locks from a finished session end on their own (10 minutes plus 2 per distraction, up to 60). On Android, blocks follow the schedules and limits you set and end when they do, or when you turn the rule off. Locks apply only to the apps you flagged.

How do I cancel or restore Pro?

Cancel in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or in iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Use Restore purchases in the app's Settings to get Pro back on a new device. See the Terms.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your device only. Delete the app to remove it (on Android you can also use Settings > Apps > Project ScaleUp > Storage > Clear storage). If your device backup is on, a copy may remain in that backup until you delete it there. See the Privacy Policy.
