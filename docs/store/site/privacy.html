Privacy Policy

Project ScaleUp for iPhone · Last updated: ⚠ DATE · Draft: have it reviewed before publishing.

Project ScaleUp ("the app") is a focus timer. This policy says what the app does with information. The short version: everything stays on your iPhone. We don't collect your data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, makes no network requests, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software. We don't receive anything about you or your device.

What the app does on your device

  • Camera. During a focus session the front camera is used to check that you're at your desk. Live video is analysed on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. No photo or video is taken, saved, uploaded or shown to anyone. The camera is off when no session is running or while a session is paused.
  • Screen Time. If you allow it, the app uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls) to notice when you open apps you chose during a session, and to lock those apps afterwards. You choose the apps in Apple's own picker. The app receives only opaque tokens and never learns which apps you picked. Your Screen Time data is not readable by the app and doesn't leave your device.
  • Your sessions. The app keeps a history on your device: when sessions happened, how long they were, which mode you used, and counts of distractions. This powers your tally, streaks and progress. It's stored in the app's private storage and an app group shared only with the app's own Screen Time extensions.
  • Away-time tracking (optional). If a future version offers it and you turn it on, the app records when your chosen apps are used outside sessions, on the device only, keeps the raw log for 30 days, and deletes it when you turn the feature off.

Sharing

We don't share, sell or transfer your information, because we don't have it.

Your choices

  • Turn the camera or Screen Time off any time in iOS Settings. The app keeps working without Screen Time; only app tracking and locking stop.
  • Deleting the app deletes its data from your device.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this changes (for example, if purchases are added and a payment provider handles purchase information), we'll update this page and the date above before the change ships.
